# Placement map

All placement is relative to Generated_Plant_Environment_N, so the surroundings follow the current plant if its position changes.

## Zone 1 - immediate site
Owned by the existing PlantEnvironmentBuilder:
- concrete plant slab and equipment pads
- service and maintenance roads
- perimeter fence
- pipe rack and utility headers
- control/utility zone
- storage containment/bunds
- nearby vessel farm, stacks, scaffolds and process-background structures
- site sign

PtMeOHEnvironmentDecorator improves this near zone with materials and selected imported details.

## Zone 2 - local/perimeter detail
Selected imported models close to the generated site:
- building-a: industrial hall
- building-f: secondary hall
- building-m: utility building
- building-t: warehouse
- detail-tank: yard/storage tanks
- light-curved: service-road lamps

These remain outside the main process focus.

## Zone 3 - mid-distance industrial estate
Generated by PtMeOHHorizonEnvironment:
- complete 360-degree ring rather than only a rear backdrop
- 32 low-poly industrial building instances
- 12 distributed storage tanks
- 20 utility stacks/columns
- industrial estate road loop and longer regional roads
- approximate radius: 120-310 m from the plant

## Zone 4 - far skyline
Also generated by PtMeOHHorizonEnvironment:
- simplified factories around all directions
- periodic tall chimneys/stacks
- overlapping vegetation/tree belts to fill skyline gaps
- approximate radius: 430-875 m

Far objects deliberately use simple geometry because only their silhouettes remain visible.

## Ground and horizon
- continuous ground plane: approximately 2400 m x 2400 m
- geometry continues beyond the atmospheric fog end
- linear fog begins around 270 m and blends fully by about 1050 m
- camera far clip is raised to at least 1800 m
- the real map edge therefore remains hidden behind atmospheric perspective

Result: rotating the camera around the plant should always show an extended industrial landscape/land horizon, not the boundary of the original PlantEnvironmentBuilder.

The environment layer never alters process calculations, solvers, UI, camera controls, flow visualization, validation or data export.
