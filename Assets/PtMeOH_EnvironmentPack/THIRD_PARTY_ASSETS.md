# Third-party asset manifest

The imported models in this pack are Kenney assets released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). Attribution is not required, but the source is recorded here for provenance.

Official sources:
- City Kit (Industrial): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial
- City Kit (Roads): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads
- CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The files were copied from the public Unity mirror repository:
https://github.com/lucrybpin/unity-community-core-city-02

Curated files:
- City Kit (Industrial): building-a.fbx
- City Kit (Industrial): building-f.fbx
- City Kit (Industrial): building-m.fbx

- City Kit (Industrial): detail-tank.fbx

- City Kit (Roads): light-curved.fbx
- City Kit (Roads): Textures/colormap.png

Why only these files:
The current project already procedurally creates the slab, roads, fences, pipe racks, utility zone, bunds, tank silhouettes, scaffolds and process-background structures. Importing the complete 100+ object source kits would add project weight without improving the PtMeOH scene. This subset adds depth and recognizable industrial context while keeping the educational visualization lightweight.

The procedural concrete/asphalt/grass/corrugated textures and panoramic sky are generated by PtMeOHEnvironmentDecorator.cs at runtime and have no third-party texture dependency.
